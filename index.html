ABOUT TESS

An automotive-focused engineering education

Tebogwana Engineering School of Specialisation (TESS) is built around a clear purpose: giving learners a real foundation in engineering, with a particular focus on automotive specialisation. Our curriculum brings together Mathematics, Science, Technology, Technical subjects and Engineering, so learners graduate with both the theory and the practical grounding the industry looks for.

Every subject at TESS is chosen to feed directly into engineering pathways — from the classroom to the workshop floor, and eventually into further study or a skilled trade.
